Plot Summary

Dom Cobb is a skilled thief who commits corporate espionage by infiltrating the subconscious of his targets. Offered a chance to have his criminal history erased, Cobb must perform an almost impossible task: plant an idea into a CEO's mind rather than steal one.

Actors

The film features an incredible ensemble cast who brought these complex dream-walkers to life.

  • Leonardo DiCaprio as Dom Cobb
  • Joseph Gordon-Levitt as Arthur
  • Elliot Page as Ariadne
  • Tom Hardy as Eames
  • Cillian Murphy as Robert Fischer

Favorite Quote

One of the most iconic lines from the movie that perfectly encapsulates the theme of questioning reality:

"You're waiting for a train. A train that will take you far away. You know where you hope this train will take you, but you can't know for sure. But it doesn't matter. Now, tell me why!"
